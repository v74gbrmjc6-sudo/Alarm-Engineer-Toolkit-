🔧 Fault Finding

FIELD CHECKLISTS
Bell tamper

Start with the event log. Establish whether the tamper occurs at rest, during bell activation, or only intermittently.

  1. Inspect external sounder lid/tamper switch and wiring.
  2. Check panel voltage at rest.
  3. Activate the bell and measure the supply again.
  4. Inspect cable joints, terminals and any junction boxes.
  5. Check bell battery condition and PCB connections.
  6. Verify the tamper return and resistor arrangement against the panel/sounder manual.
  7. Retest repeatedly and confirm the fault restores correctly.

Useful clue: if the fault appears immediately after a bell activation and restores quickly, investigate the bell supply/load, battery and tamper circuit rather than assuming the panel is faulty.

Zone tamper

Confirm whether the fault is permanent or intermittent, then isolate device/cable/panel systematically.

  1. Check event history and identify the exact zone.
  2. Inspect detector lid, tamper switch and terminals.
  3. Confirm the programmed wiring type (EOL/DEOL/TEOL as applicable).
  4. Measure resistance at the device and at the panel.
  5. Wiggle-test the cable and inspect joints.
  6. Temporarily substitute/test in accordance with the manufacturer's procedure.
  7. Restore the original circuit and retest.
Zone false alarm
  1. Check event history for pattern/time.
  2. Identify detector type and environment.
  3. Inspect for insects, cobwebs, heat sources, drafts, sunlight, loose mounting and vibration.
  4. Check detector supply voltage.
  5. Check cable and connections.
  6. Check programming and sensitivity/attributes where supported.
  7. Test the detector under controlled conditions.
Mains / battery fault
  1. Confirm mains status and PSU indication.
  2. Measure panel charging/supply voltage according to manufacturer specification.
  3. Inspect battery terminals and fuse.
  4. Check battery age and physical condition.
  5. Measure under appropriate load/test conditions.
  6. Check total auxiliary load if voltage is low.
Voltage drop

Measure at the panel and at the device under load. Calculate the difference. Investigate cable resistance, cable length, connections, conductor size and load/current demand. Do not use a generic pass/fail voltage as a substitute for the panel/device manufacturer's specification.

ATS / ARC / communications fault
  1. Read the exact event text and timestamp.
  2. Identify the communication path: IP, 4G/GSM, PSTN or other.
  3. Check panel communicator status and power.
  4. Check network/SIM/connectivity indicators where applicable.
  5. Check signal and polling/heartbeat status where supported.
  6. Confirm ARC account/path configuration with the monitoring provider.
  7. Perform the manufacturer's approved communications test.
Wireless / Ricochet device missing
  1. Check device battery and tamper.
  2. Check last-seen/signal information.
  3. Check device location and obstructions.
  4. Check wireless network topology/expander status.
  5. Use manufacturer software/tools where available.
  6. Re-learn only using the correct manufacturer procedure.
RS485 / bus / expander fault
  1. Identify the exact bus device/address.
  2. Check supply at the device.
  3. Inspect polarity and cable continuity.
  4. Check topology and termination requirements for the specific system.
  5. Check addressing and duplicate addresses.
  6. Isolate sections systematically and follow the manufacturer's bus procedure.
Intermittent fault

Record exact time, weather/temperature if relevant, system state, recent events and what action causes the fault. Test mechanically (movement/vibration), electrically (voltage/resistance) and by substitution only where the manufacturer procedure permits.

Safety: this is a troubleshooting aid. Verify the exact panel/model/firmware and manufacturer's instructions before changing programming, wiring or protection circuits. Follow safe electrical working practices.

Commissioning checklist
  1. Confirm panel/model/firmware.
  2. Confirm zones and wiring types.
  3. Confirm detector operation and tamper circuits.
  4. Test internal/external sounders and strobes.
  5. Test mains and standby battery.
  6. Test communications/ARC path where applicable.
  7. Check time/date and event log.
  8. Test user operation.
  9. Complete required signalling and alarm tests.
  10. Document final condition and any recommendations.
