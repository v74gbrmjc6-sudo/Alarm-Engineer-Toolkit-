Wiring & Diagrams

Standard NC circuit

Detector contacts are normally closed. A break in the circuit can be interpreted as an alarm depending on the panel programming.

EOL

One end-of-line resistor is used at the detector/end of the circuit. The panel must be programmed for the matching wiring type and resistor value.

DEOL

Two resistor states allow the panel to distinguish alarm and tamper. Always place resistors as specified by the panel manufacturer.

TEOL / Anti-mask

Triple-end-of-line arrangements can provide alarm, tamper and fault/anti-mask states where supported.

Never assume resistor values between manufacturers. Verify the panel's installation manual before changing field wiring.

Commissioning checklist

Confirm panel model and firmware → verify zone wiring type → test every device → test sounders → check battery and PSU voltage → test communications/ATS → review event log → record final configuration.

Credential recovery

Site Mode

1 · Identify

Record panel, firmware, symptoms and event-log sequence before changing anything.

2 · Isolate

Determine whether the issue is device, cable, power, network/bus, sounder or communicator related.

3 · Measure

Use a meter where appropriate. Record voltage/resistance before and during the fault.

4 · Prove

Test the suspected component/circuit and confirm the fault clears and remains stable.

5 · Document

Record work carried out, parts used, outstanding issues and recommendations.
